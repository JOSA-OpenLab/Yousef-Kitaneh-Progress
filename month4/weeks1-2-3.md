### Month 04, Weeks0{1..3}: Reflection

This month was defined by a lot of research and studying, as I try to prepare
for the mentorship in order to make the most out of it.

I've also been balancing my time between preparing for the mentorship, finding
contribution opportunities, and balancing an internship this summer, so in the
OpenLab sphere, I've been focusing on the following topics:

#### Embedded Rust

I've been reading and going through a lot of embedded Rust resources, such as
the [embedonomicon](https://docs.rust-embedded.org/embedonomicon/), Dr.Omar's
[Simplified Embedded Rust](https://www.theembeddedrustacean.com/ser-esp-no-std),
and for esp32 specifically, the [esp32 implrust](https://esp32.implrust.com/).

#### Constrained Microcontrollers

I've been tinkering around with an UNO R3 arduino board, to prepare more for the
"traditonal" embedded experience, i.e., working with C/C++ in favor of Rust.
I've been also revising my electric physics courses & labs, and want to start
working with heavier and more powerful microcontrollers, but I will probably
need to do that with the help of virtualizers. Specifically, I want to begin
working with QEMU and Wokwi environments.

#### Branching out: Compilers and Computational Linguistics

Outside of the embedded sphere, I've been exploring compilers and computational
linguistics, mostly because I want to diversify my skillset. And this domain is
one I find interesting and still related to my skillset. A project I want to
contribute to soon is [perses](https://github.com/uw-pluverse/perses), which is
a language agnostic program reduction framework. Also, a significant part of it
is written in Rust, C, and C++, what a coincidence!

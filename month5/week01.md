### Month05: Week01

This week marks my first week of mentorship with my mentor. We had our initial
meeting where we discussed the overarching goals for the mentorship, which is
focused around introducing OpenTitan support into ZephyrOS. I am very excited
about this opportunity as it aligns perfectly with my interests in embedded
systems and open-source software development, and will prepare me for my future
goal of working on adding Rust support into ZephyrOS as well.

I've been given the following subjects to research during my first week:

1. **The ZephyrOS Hardware Description Model**

   - This involves understanding what a DeviceTree data structure is, why and
     how it is used in ZephyrOS, and it's various components and forms.
2. **The OpenTitan mission and its role**
   - What is a root of trust, why should this be open-source, and what is
     currently missing to drive OpenTitan support into ZephyrOS.
3. **Various utility functions in ZephyrOS**
   - technologies such as cmake, kconfig, and west, the device interface
     function, and the bare metal C-logic interface on OpenTitan.
